Workflow per novel

  1. Intake. Match the EPUB, MP3 and new cover to the ISBN list.
  2. EPUB edits. Cover, page 2, hidden image.
  3. Audio edit. Remove the phrase and export at the same quality.
  4. Automated checks on every file: text match, EPUBCheck, audio duration.
  5. Manual review. Cover, page 2, start and end of every audio file; the first novel of each series in full.
  6. Packaging. One folder per series, a delivery sheet and a weekly progress report.

Needed from management by 14 Nov

  • Master list of the 30 series and 797 novels with ISBNs
  • All source files (EPUB + MP3) in one place
  • New covers, or each batch’s covers one week ahead
  • Sign-off on the 5 delivered novels as the final spec
  • One reviewer, and the delivery location (Drive or server)

A batch counts as approved if no feedback arrives within 2 working days.
